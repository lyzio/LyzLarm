Sleep tracking. Alarms still ring at the exact time you set; the smart wake window arrives in v0.3.

- **Start sleep** at bedtime, put the phone on the mattress: LyzLarm records movement all night
- Morning screen with a movement graph, your alarm, and any gaps in tracking
- Past nights in the Sleep tab; export any night as CSV
- Bedtime reminder and low-battery warning (Settings › Tracking)
- Tracking survives battery savers, the app being closed, and restarts
- New logo: the moon holds the sun
- Still no internet permission, no accounts, no tracking of you beyond this phone

Install: download `LyzLarm-0.2.0.apk` below on your phone and open it (installs over 0.1). Android 8.0 or newer.
