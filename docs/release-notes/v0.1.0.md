First release: a reliable alarm. Sleep tracking arrives in v0.2.

- Reliable alarms that ring on time, also after a restart, a time-zone change or an app update
- Full-screen alarm over the lock screen, vibration first, gentle fade-in
- Fixed snooze (9 min by default)
- Setup that tests the alarm on your phone, plus a System check in Settings
- No internet permission, no accounts, no tracking

Install: download `LyzLarm-0.1.0.apk` below on your phone and open it. Android 8.0 or newer.
