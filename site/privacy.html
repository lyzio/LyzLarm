Privacy policy

Anatole – Sleep tracker & alarm, Android app se.lyzio.anatole. Effective 27 November 2026.

Anatole collects nothing and shares nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere. Everything it records stays on your phone.

Permissions

  • No internet. Anatole does not request the INTERNET permission. Every build is checked for this automatically.
  • Camera, only if you turn on the barcode Snooze lock challenge. Camera images are analyzed live on the phone to read the code and are never saved.
  • Notifications, alarms, full-screen alarm, foreground service: so the alarm rings reliably and night tracking keeps running while the screen is off.
  • Motion sensor (no permission prompt): movement while you track a night.

The current version does not use the microphone. If a future version does, this policy will be updated first, and sound will be analyzed live without recording.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting services and no Google Play Services in the app. Android cloud backup is turned off for Anatole, so your data is not copied to Google's servers by the app.

Export and import

You can export a night as a CSV file. This only happens when you tap Export, and the file goes where you choose (for example a folder or an app you share it with). Imported files are read on the phone only.

Deleting your data

Long-press a night in Past nights to delete it. Uninstalling Anatole, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes everything it stored.

Children

Anatole is meant for adults (18+).

Health information

Anatole estimates sleep from movement. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition.
