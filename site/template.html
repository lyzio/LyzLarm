Wake up at your lightest sleep.

LyzLarm is a smart alarm for Android that tracks your sleep on the phone and rings at the right moment in your wake window. It never touches the internet: your nights stay yours.

Private by design

No internet permission

The app can't send anything anywhere. Anyone can check this in the source code.

Nothing recorded

Sound is analyzed live and thrown away. Only numbers are saved.

No accounts, no ads

Your data stays on your phone unless you export it yourself.

Install

  1. Download the APK from the latest release on your phone.
  2. Open it and allow your browser or file manager to install apps once.
  3. Open LyzLarm and follow the setup, including the test alarm.
